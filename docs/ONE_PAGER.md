# MilMed Connect PitchBook — One-Pager

## The problem

Dual-use medtech discovery is fragmented across multiple public sources. MILMED Connect's founders currently research companies manually across MTEC, SBIR, USAspending, and related sites. A company can appear in one source and be invisible in another.

## The solution

**MilMed Connect PitchBook** — an add-on to MILMED's existing AetherForge (data foundation: ~21,800 companies, ~102,000 awards) and Signal Registry (Helix's deep profiles and DU-T2). It adds the MTEC directory and membership, a quick dual-use pre-screen aligned with DU-T2, and a read-only MCP connection. Local on the MILMED Connect iMac, MIT-licensed.

- One search layer over validated sources, with conservative entity resolution and evidence links on every claim.
- Dual-use scoring with an explicit "unknown" state when evidence is missing. Optional AI acceleration (Jev or LLM) when keys are available and usable.
- Answers cross-source questions that currently require manual research, with cited evidence.

## Positioning

"An add-on to AetherForge and Signal Registry that brings in MTEC and a quick dual-use pre-screen, so focused military-medical questions can be answered across thousands of companies — with DU-T2 still the deep assessment." Not a complete PitchBook replacement, and not a fixed annual savings claim until the core is validated with Eric and Kirtly.

## What it is not

Not a replacement for MILMED Connect's relationships or judgment. The platform surfaces candidates and evidence; the phone call still comes from the person.

## The ask

Phase 0: agree on three demo questions with manually verified expected answers (including an ambiguous match and an honest "insufficient evidence" result), confirm AetherForge + Signal Registry as the foundation, then add MTEC and the pre-screen. About seven-week commitment for the validated core. Extension features are separately estimated.
